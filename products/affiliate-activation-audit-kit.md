## Part 1 — The Activation Scorecard

Score your program 0-2 on each line (0 = not doing this, 1 = doing it inconsistently, 2 = doing it well). Add up your total out of 40.

### Onboarding (0-10)
1. New affiliates receive more than just a welcome email and a login
2. There's a defined first-30-days sequence for every new partner
3. New affiliates are told specifically what's converting right now, not just given a generic link
4. Affiliates get a real contact person, not just a support inbox
5. Expectations are set clearly (what good performance looks like, what support is available)

### Follow-Up Cadence (0-10)
1. Partners who haven't sent traffic in 14 days get a check-in
2. There's a defined cadence for dormant partner outreach (not ad hoc)
3. Top-performing partners get different, higher-touch treatment than the long tail
4. Affiliates hear from you when there's something new to promote, not just at renewal time
5. There's a process for re-engaging affiliates who went fully dark for 60+ days

### Payout Competitiveness (0-10)
1. You know what competitors in your space pay affiliates
2. Your commission structure is at or above the median for your category
3. Payout terms (cookie window, payment schedule, minimum threshold) are competitive
4. There's a tiered structure that rewards your best partners more
5. Payment is reliable and on schedule — no chasing required from affiliates

### Creative & Proof (0-10)
1. Creative assets (banners, swipe copy, landing pages) have been updated in the last 6 months
2. Affiliates have access to real performance proof (case studies, testimonials, conversion data)
3. There's more than one creative angle available — not a single banner pack
4. Affiliates can request custom creative or messaging help
5. Top affiliates' best-performing creative gets shared back with the rest of the program

**Scoring guide:**
- 32-40: Strong program. Focus on segmentation and scaling what's working.
- 20-31: Real gaps. 1-2 of the four categories are likely leaking partners.
- Below 20: Activation problem confirmed. Most of your approved partners are probably dormant.

## Part 3 — Payout Benchmark Reference

*Directional ranges based on common market structures — always verify against current competitor offers in your specific niche before finalizing.*

| Category | Typical Commission | Cookie Window | Notes |
|----------|--------------------|--------------|-------|
| SaaS (subscription) | 20-30% recurring, first 12mo | 30-60 days | Recurring commission is the norm and the expectation |
| Creator tools / platforms | 20-30% recurring or flat $20-50/sale | 30-60 days | Flat-rate works well for lower-priced products |
| Course / info products | 30-50% one-time | 30-60 days | Higher rates standard due to high margins |
| Subscription boxes / e-commerce | 10-20% one-time | 14-30 days | Lower margins mean lower affiliate payout ceiling |
| High-ticket services | Flat fee or % of first invoice | 60-90 days | Longer sales cycles need longer cookie windows |

**Rule of thumb:** if your payout sits meaningfully below this range for your category, that alone may explain low activation — serious affiliates compare offers before deciding where to spend their traffic.

## Part 4 — Reading Your Score: What to Fix First

1. **If Onboarding scored lowest:** this is usually the highest-leverage fix. A partner who never got a real onboarding sequence will stay dormant regardless of how good your payout is.
2. **If Follow-Up Cadence scored lowest:** you likely have partners worth reactivating right now — use the templates in Part 2 starting with anyone dormant 14-60 days; that group converts back to active fastest.
3. **If Payout scored lowest:** benchmark against Part 3 before changing anything else — a below-market payout will undercut every other fix.
4. **If Creative & Proof scored lowest:** this is the easiest and cheapest fix — refreshing assets and surfacing real proof points usually takes a day, not a quarter.

If two or more categories scored below 5, that's a sign the issue is systemic rather than a single fix — that's the point where a full Affiliate Program Audit (which maps the entire partner lifecycle, not just these four areas) becomes worth the investment.

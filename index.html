3.0

Most programs do not need more affiliates.

They need the ones they already have to start sending traffic. After two decades inside affiliate programs, the same five defects show up again and again, and almost none of them are fixed by recruiting harder.

  1. 3.1

    Activation stalls at approval

    Partners sign up, get one automated email, and never send a click.

  2. 3.2

    Payouts are quietly uncompetitive

    Serious affiliates compare terms. Flat, untiered deals lose the best partners to rivals.

  3. 3.3

    No retention or reactivation system

    Affiliates send one batch of traffic and vanish, and nobody notices.

  4. 3.4

    The top 5% never get approached

    The partners who would actually move revenue are not in the program at all.

  5. 3.5

    Tracking, creatives and onboarding leak

    Conversions you already paid for go unattributed or unconverted.

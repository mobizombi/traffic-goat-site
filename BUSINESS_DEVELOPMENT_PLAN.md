# Traffic Goat - Business Development Sprint

## Goal
Get the first 1-2 Traffic Goat clients by offering free Revenue Leak Audits to AI and SaaS founders, then converting the strongest audits into paid implementation or advisory work.

## 30-Day Targets
- Build a list of 150 qualified AI/SaaS prospects.
- Send 80 personalized outbound messages.
- Book 8-12 audit conversations.
- Deliver 5-8 useful written audits.
- Send 3-5 paid proposals.
- Close 1-2 clients.

## Ideal First Customer
Focus on AI/SaaS companies with visible traction and a likely revenue system gap.

Good signals:
- Active website with pricing, demo, signup, or lead capture.
- Founder, CEO, growth lead, or partnerships lead visible on LinkedIn or X.
- Recent launch, funding, newsletter mention, Product Hunt activity, or content momentum.
- Existing traffic, partner potential, affiliate mention, marketplace listing, or manual sales process.

Avoid for now:
- Pre-product startups.
- Local service businesses.
- Ecommerce stores.
- Companies with no clear monetization path.

## Offer
Lead with a free Revenue Leak Audit.

The audit should identify 3-5 leaks across:
- Funnel and conversion flow.
- Affiliate or partner activation.
- Pricing, packaging, or monetization logic.
- AI workflow and manual ops drag.
- Follow-up, reporting, and lead handling.

End each audit with one recommended paid next step.

## Weekly Rhythm
Spend 5-7 hours per week.

Daily:
- Add 5-10 prospects to the tracker.
- Send 3-5 personalized messages.
- Follow up with active conversations.

Weekly:
- Publish 3 LinkedIn posts.
- Deliver 1-2 audits.
- Send proposals to the best-fit prospects.
- Review reply rate, booked calls, and proposal movement.

## Paid Offers
Use simple fixed offers first.

### Revenue Leak Fix Sprint
Price: $1,500-$3,500

For a focused 1-2 week implementation sprint that fixes the highest-impact leak found in the audit.

### Growth Infrastructure Retainer
Price: $2,500-$6,000/month

For ongoing affiliate systems, AI workflows, funnel fixes, reporting, and partnership execution.

### Strategic Advisory
Price: $750-$1,500/month

For lighter founder/operator support when the prospect is not ready for implementation.

## Follow-Up Sequence
Day 0: Personalized audit offer.

Day 3: Follow up with one specific observed leak.

Day 7: Send a useful insight or mini teardown.

Day 14: Close the loop politely.

## Weekly Review
Track:
- Prospects added.
- Messages sent.
- Replies.
- Positive replies.
- Calls booked.
- Audits delivered.
- Proposals sent.
- Closed clients.

Adjust if reply rate is below 8% after 40 messages:
- Narrow the ICP.
- Make the first line more specific.
- Lead with one concrete observed leak.

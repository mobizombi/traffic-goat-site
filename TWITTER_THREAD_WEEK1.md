1/
A client came to me convinced their affiliate program needed more partners.

230 approved affiliates. 14 sending traffic.

The data said the problem wasn't recruitment. It was activation.

Here's what was actually broken 🧵

2/
Onboarding ended at "approved."

One automated email. A login link. Nothing else.

No partner converts traffic into sales without knowing what's working, what to say, or who to ask. Silence reads as "this program isn't a priority" — so it got treated like one.

3/
Payout was 15% one-time. Category median: 20-30% recurring.

Any affiliate serious enough to compare offers was choosing competitors by default.

The program was unintentionally selecting for the least-informed, least-active partners.

4/
Zero follow-up system for dormant partners.

216 inactive affiliates just sitting in a dashboard. Nobody flagged them. Nobody re-engaged them.

Some percentage of "dormant" isn't gone — it's waiting for a reason. Left alone, that reason never comes.

5/
The fix wasn't a recruitment push. It was three things:

→ 5-touch onboarding sequence, first 21 days
→ Moved to 25% recurring, tiered to 30% for top performers
→ 3-touch reactivation sequence at 14/30/60 days dormant

6/
60 days later:

Active affiliates: 14 → 41
No new affiliates recruited.

Every gain came from activating partners who were already there.

7/
If your affiliate program feels stuck, check this before you recruit harder:

How many of your approved partners have sent a click in the last 30 days?

If that number is small relative to your total, you don't have a traffic problem. You have an activation problem.

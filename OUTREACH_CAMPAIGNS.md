# Traffic Goat - Outreach Campaigns

## Campaign 1 - AI/SaaS Founder Revenue Leak Audit

### Target
AI/SaaS founders with:
- live website
- pricing, demo, signup, or waitlist
- recent launch or content activity
- visible founder on LinkedIn

### First Message
Hey [Name],

I looked at [Company] and noticed a possible revenue leak around [specific area: partner activation / follow-up / funnel monetization / manual ops].

I run Traffic Goat, where I help online businesses find and fix leaks between traffic, funnels, affiliates, AI workflows, and monetization.

Want me to send over 2-3 quick observations?

### Follow-Up 1 - Day 3
Hey [Name],

One specific thing I would check at [Company]:

[Specific observation].

That can leak revenue because [short reason].

Worth a quick teardown?

### Follow-Up 2 - Day 7
Closing the loop here.

If improving revenue efficiency across traffic, follow-up, partners, or monetization becomes a priority, I can send a quick Revenue Leak Audit.

## Campaign 2 - Affiliate Program Activation

### Target
Businesses with:
- affiliate page
- partner program
- creator/referral program
- marketplace listings
- visible partner/channel motion

### First Message
Hey [Name],

I noticed [Company] has a partner/affiliate angle.

One common leak I see: programs recruit partners, but the activation path after signup is too weak. Partners join, then do not know the first campaign, first audience, or first asset to use.

Want me to take a quick look and send 2-3 activation fixes?

### Follow-Up 1 - Day 3
Quick thought on [Company]:

If partners are not activating, the issue is usually not motivation. It is missing momentum.

I would check onboarding, asset clarity, first-win path, and follow-up timing.

Want me to map that out?

## Campaign 3 - AI Workflow Drag

### Target
Lean teams doing sales, growth, content, reporting, or partner ops manually.

### First Message
Hey [Name],

I liked what you are building with [Company].

One thing I would look for is manual revenue drag: lead review, follow-up, reporting, partner tracking, and content repurposing still happening by hand.

That is usually where AI workflows create practical leverage.

Want me to send a quick audit angle?

### Follow-Up 1 - Day 3
The highest-value AI workflow is usually not content generation.

It is the repetitive operational work that delays revenue conversations.

For [Company], I would first check [specific workflow].

Worth a quick teardown?

## Campaign 4 - Monetization Leak

### Target
Companies with traffic/content but unclear revenue depth.

### First Message
Hey [Name],

I reviewed [Company] and noticed a possible monetization leak around [specific offer / CTA / funnel step].

Sometimes the funnel converts, but does not monetize deeply enough because the next step, follow-up, or offer sequence is weak.

Want me to send a quick Revenue Leak Audit?

## Personalization Blocks

Use one per message.

### Pricing/Funnel
I noticed the path from [landing page / pricing / demo] to the next revenue step may be doing too much work without enough follow-up.

### Affiliate
I noticed the partner angle, but I would want to see whether partners get a clear first action after signup.

### AI Ops
I noticed a lot of potential for AI-assisted follow-up/reporting, especially if the team is lean.

### Partnership
I noticed partnership potential, but it is unclear whether there is a tracked partner motion behind it.

## Qualification Checklist
Add a prospect only if at least 3 are true:
- live business
- clear monetization path
- founder/growth lead visible
- recent activity
- likely traffic or partner motion
- website shows funnel, pricing, demo, signup, or lead capture
- AI/SaaS/subscription/affiliate relevance
